Explanation: The predicate structures match, unifying variable C with a and variable B with d.

Explanation: Unification is symmetric; variables C and B bind to a and d respectively.

Explanation: The variable A cannot simultaneously unify with two distinct atoms, b and c.

?- A = B, B = f.Result: A = f, B = f.   
Explanation: A unifies with B, and assigning f to B binds both variables to atom f.
